# Netflix SQL Analytics – Business Insights Project<br>
Logo

## Overview

This project delivers a comprehensive SQL-based analysis of Netflix movies and TV shows data. The objective is to extract actionable insights by performing structured data exploration, querying, and analytical processing. The analysis focuses on solving defined business problems through data-driven techniques. This README documents the project objectives, business questions addressed, methodologies applied, key analytical findings, and overall conclusions derived from the dataset.

## Objectives
- Analyzed the distribution of content types, specifically comparing Movies and TV Shows, to understand platform content composition.

- Identified and evaluated the most frequently occurring content ratings across both Movies and TV Shows to assess audience targeting and classification trends.

- Performed analytical breakdowns of content based on release years, countries of production, and duration metrics to uncover temporal, geographical, and structural patterns.

- Explored and categorized content using defined criteria and keyword-based analysis to enable deeper content segmentation and insight generation.
